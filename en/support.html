Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Where is my data stored?
A. All data is stored locally on your device. There are no uploads to external servers.
Q. Do I need to create an account?
A. No, account registration is not required. You can start using the app immediately after download.
Q. How many photos can I add per drink?
A. You can add up to 5 photos per drink entry.
Q. What types of drinks can I record?
A. You can record Wine, Sake, Beer, Whiskey, and Others categories.
Q. Can I backup my data?
A. Backup functionality is not available in the current version. We plan to add this feature in a future update.
Q. Can I transfer data when changing devices?
A. Data transfer is not available in the current version. We plan to add this feature in a future update.
Q. Is the app free?
A. Yes, Sake & Wine is free to use.

User Guide

Recording a Drink

  1. Tap the "Post" tab at the bottom of the screen
  2. Select a photo from camera or library
  3. Enter name and category (required)
  4. Add your rating (1-5 stars)
  5. Enter brewery, origin, tasting notes (optional)
  6. Tap "Save"

Searching for Drinks

  1. Tap the "Search" tab at the bottom
  2. Enter keywords in the search box
  3. Filter by category or sort options

Editing or Deleting

  1. Tap a drink from home screen or search results
  2. Select "Edit" or "Delete" on the detail screen

System Requirements

  • iOS: iOS 13.0 or later
  • Android: Android 8.0 or later
