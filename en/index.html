Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how sake-wine (hereinafter referred to as "the App") handles personal information. Please read this policy carefully before using the App.

Information We Collect

The App may collect the following information to provide our services:

  • Beverage Records: Information about alcoholic beverages entered by users, including brand names, types, ratings, and notes
  • Image Data: Photos of beverages taken or uploaded by users
  • Location Information: Location data of establishments where beverages were purchased or consumed (only with user permission)

How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used for the following purposes:

  • Providing beverage recording and management features
  • Displaying establishment information and map functionality
  • Improving user experience and enhancing our services
  • Creating statistical data (in a non-personally identifiable format)

Data Storage

Information entered in the App is generally stored on the user's device. If cloud service integration features are used, we will clearly indicate this and obtain user consent beforehand.

Third-Party Disclosure

The App does not disclose collected personal information to third parties except in the following cases:

  • When user consent has been obtained
  • When required by law
  • When necessary to protect life, body, or property

External Services

The App may use the following external services. Please refer to each company's website for their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Places API (for obtaining establishment information)
  • Google Maps (for map display)

Security

The App implements appropriate security measures to protect user information. However, we cannot guarantee that information transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for individuals under the legal drinking age in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be changed as necessary. If there are significant changes, we will notify users within the App.
